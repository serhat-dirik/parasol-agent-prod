This demo runs against the *Portal secured* tab. The same overnight storm from Demo 1 beat 4, now on the platform. It is refused, named, and survivable, and the morning report is two screens you already have.

[NOTE]
.Know: budgets and alerts with a name
====
In Demo 1 the storm drained a shared key and nobody knew. Here the spend has a name, the limit is per user, and the alert carries the user. Key message 4.
====

== Beat 1: the storm starts

. The customer script runs against the secured portal at "02:00", sped up to a few minutes. It signs in as *tom.becker* and loops long off-topic requests.
+
What you should see: the storm begins, but only tom.becker's own budget is at risk.

== Beat 2: refused and rate-limited, while Rebecca keeps working

. Watch the chat as *tom.becker*. The off-topic request gets a polite topic refusal from the guardrail.
+
What you should see: a courteous message declining the off-topic request.
+
image::demo3-01-tombecker-refusal.png[tom.becker off-topic request gets a polite topic refusal in chat,link=self,window=blank]

. The loop keeps hammering. After the per-user token limit, the chat changes.
+
What you should see: "you have reached your usage limit (429)". The limit is bound to tom.becker, not the assistant.
+
image::demo3-02-tombecker-429.png[tom.becker hits the per-user limit, chat shows usage limit 429,link=self,window=blank]

. In another session, *rebecca* keeps working.
+
What you should see: rebecca gets normal answers throughout the storm. Her budget was never tom.becker's budget.
+
image::demo3-03-rebecca-working.png[rebecca in another session still gets normal answers during the storm,link=self,window=blank]

[NOTE]
.Know: the sandbox has a front door
====
The limit stopped one user, not the service. tom.becker is capped; rebecca is unaffected. Identity-bound, not a blanket wall. Key message 5.
====

[NOTE]
.Know: revoke before you kill
====
Disabling the one user is the precise action. Taking the whole assistant offline is the blunt one. Reach for the precise control first, and keep the blunt one ready. The morning report is two screens you already have: the alert and the usage dashboard.
====
