title: Claims line opening hours and public holidays 2026
classification: public
---
The Parasol claims line is open Sunday to Thursday from 08:00 to 20:00 and Friday and Saturday from 09:00 to 17:00. Outside those hours the night service desk takes new claims and urgent roadside requests.

Branches are closed on public holidays. On those days new claims can still be filed in the customer portal and are picked up on the next working day.

Roadside assistance is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
