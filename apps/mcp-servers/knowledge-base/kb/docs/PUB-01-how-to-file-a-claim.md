title: How to file a claim with Parasol Insurance
classification: public
---
This guide explains the claims process for every Parasol customer and every member of staff who helps customers, including contractors on the service desk.

A claim can be filed in the Parasol customer portal, by phone on the claims line, or at a branch. The customer needs the policy number, the date and place of the incident, a short description of what happened, and photos of the damage where possible. For motor claims a police report number is required when another vehicle or a third party is involved.

After a claim is filed it receives a claim number of the form CLM-1234 and is assigned to an adjuster. The adjuster reviews the documents, may ask for a repair estimate from an approved workshop, and recommends a settlement. Payouts above the adjuster's authority are approved by a claims manager.

Customers can follow the status of their claim in the portal. The usual statuses are Submitted, In Review, Approved, Denied and Paid.
