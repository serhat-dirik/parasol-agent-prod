id: PUB-03
title: Customer service standards and response times
classification: public
---
Parasol commits to the following service standards for every customer contact.

Phone calls to the claims line are answered within 3 minutes on average. Emails and portal messages are answered within 1 working day. A new claim is acknowledged within 24 hours and assigned to an adjuster within 2 working days.

Complaints are acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 15 working days. A customer who is not satisfied with the outcome can ask for a review by the customer relations team.

Service desk staff, including contractors working the evening and night shifts, follow these standards and record every contact in the claims portal.
