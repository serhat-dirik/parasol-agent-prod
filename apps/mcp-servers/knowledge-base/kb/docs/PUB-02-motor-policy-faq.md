title: Motor policy FAQ - deductibles, cover and windscreen repairs
classification: public
---
What is a deductible? The deductible is the part of a claim the customer pays. Parasol motor policies carry a standard deductible of AED 500 for own-damage claims, and AED 250 for windscreen-only claims.

What does comprehensive cover include? Comprehensive cover pays for damage to the customer's own car from collision, fire, theft and flood, and for third-party liability. Third-party-only cover pays for damage to other people's property and injury to others, not for the customer's own car.

Can I choose my own garage? Customers may use any workshop on Parasol's approved repair network. Repairs at a workshop outside the network are reimbursed up to the network rate.

How long does a payout take? Once a claim is approved, Parasol pays within 10 working days.
