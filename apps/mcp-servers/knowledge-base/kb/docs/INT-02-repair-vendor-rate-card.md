title: Repair-vendor rate card 2026 (internal)
classification: internal
---
Internal - Parasol claims staff only. Negotiated rates with the approved repair network for 2026.

Labour: AED 180 per hour for mechanical work, AED 160 per hour for body work, AED 140 per hour for paint preparation. Paint materials are reimbursed at cost plus 10 percent.

Windscreen replacement: AED 900 for standard saloon cars, AED 1,400 for SUVs, AED 2,200 for windscreens with driver-assistance cameras including recalibration.

Courtesy car: AED 120 per day, up to 10 days, only when the policy includes a courtesy car.

Workshops that exceed these rates without prior approval are paid at the rate card and receive a written notice; three notices in a year lead to a review of the workshop's network status.
