title: Repair-vendor rate card 2026 (internal)
classification: internal
---
Internal - Parasol claims staff only. Negotiated rates with the approved repair network for 2026.

Labour: USD 180 per hour for mechanical work, USD 160 per hour for body work, USD 140 per hour for paint preparation. Paint materials are reimbursed at cost plus 10 percent.

Windscreen replacement: USD 900 for standard saloon cars, USD 1,400 for SUVs, USD 2,200 for windscreens with driver-assistance cameras including recalibration.

Courtesy car: USD 120 per day, up to 10 days, only when the policy includes a courtesy car.

Workshops that exceed these rates without prior approval are paid at the rate card and receive a written notice; three notices in a year lead to a review of the workshop's network status.
