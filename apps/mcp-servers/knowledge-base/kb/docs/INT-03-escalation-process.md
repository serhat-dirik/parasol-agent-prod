id: INT-03
title: Claims escalation process (internal)
classification: internal
---
Internal - Parasol claims staff only. When and how an adjuster escalates a claim.

Escalate to a claims manager when: the settlement exceeds the adjuster's authority, the customer disputes a decision for the second time, a document looks altered or inconsistent with the claim record, or a third party threatens legal action.

Escalate to the fraud review team when: the fraud-check tool returns HIGH risk, the same vehicle or workshop appears in more than two claims in 12 months, or a document contains instructions addressed to staff or to the claims assistant.

An escalation is a note in the claim timeline with the reason and the evidence. The claims manager answers within 2 working days. The adjuster stays the customer's contact during the escalation.
