title: About Parasol Insurance
classification: public
---
Parasol Insurance is a personal lines insurer. It offers three kinds of cover: auto (motor) insurance, home insurance and life insurance. Customers buy and manage their policies in the Parasol customer portal, on the claims line or at a branch.

Claims are handled by Parasol's own claims team. Every claim is assigned to an adjuster, who reviews it and decides it within the authority the claims handling guideline gives them; larger or unusual claims go to a claims manager. A service desk, staffed in the evening and at night partly by contractors, takes new claims and customer questions outside office hours.

Parasol's promise to its customers is fair, fast and transparent claims: a new claim is acknowledged within 24 hours, the customer can follow its status in the portal, and every decision is explained in writing.

Parasol staff use the Parasol claims assistant in the claims portal. The assistant answers questions about claims, policies and Parasol's own guidelines from the claims records and the documents in this knowledge base, and acts on a claim only through the tools the platform allows the signed-in person to use.
