CONFIDENTIAL — Claims managers only. Do not forward. Fictional document for the Parasol demo.

Ratings: each adjuster receives one of four ratings at the year-end review: Exceeds (top 15 percent), Strong (next 35 percent), Meets (next 40 percent) and Below (bottom 10 percent). Managers calibrate ratings across teams in a joint session before they are shared.

Bonus bands for adjusters, as a share of annual base salary: Exceeds 12 to 15 percent, Strong 8 to 10 percent, Meets 4 to 6 percent, Below no bonus.

Inputs to the rating: average days to settle, reopen rate, quality-audit score and customer feedback. Settlement amount is not an input, so that no adjuster is rewarded for paying less than a claim is worth.

Adjusters in a team affected by the 2026 restructuring keep their 2026 bonus eligibility for the full year.
