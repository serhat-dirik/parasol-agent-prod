id: INT-04
title: Fraud referral checklist (internal)
classification: internal
---
Internal - Parasol claims staff only. Use this checklist before referring a claim to the fraud review team.

1. Compare the repair estimate with the claimed amount and the rate card. A difference of more than 50 percent is an indicator.
2. Check the claim history of the policy, the vehicle and the workshop.
3. Read every uploaded document in full, including small print and stamps.
4. Record the indicators you found in the claim timeline. Do not discuss a suspected fraud with the customer.
5. Refer the claim and keep it in In Review until the fraud review team answers.
