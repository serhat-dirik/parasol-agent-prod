CONFIDENTIAL — Claims managers only. Do not forward. Fictional document for the Parasol demo.

Summary: from the third quarter of 2026 Parasol consolidates its three motor adjuster teams into two. The work of Motor Claims Team B, led by Rebecca Torres, moves to Team A (lead Omar Haddad) and Team C (lead Priya Nair). The plan reduces adjuster positions from 18 to 14.

Roles affected: the four positions in scope are in Motor Claims Team B. Team B members will be offered redeployment to the new fraud review desk or to the commercial claims team first; voluntary separation packages are offered to those who do not take a new role. The team lead role of Team B ends with the merger.

Timeline: managers are briefed in May 2026. Individual consultations with Team B start on 1 July 2026. The new structure takes effect on 1 October 2026.

Communication: nothing is shared with adjusters before the consultations start. Questions go to Marcus Johnson, claims operations manager.
